Frequently Asked Questions

What is knowledge base software?

Knowledge base software is a tool for creating, organizing, and publishing self-service documentation. It allows customers to find answers without contacting support, reducing ticket volume and improving satisfaction.

How much does knowledge base software cost?

Prices range from free (Confluence, Notion) to $20-100+/seat/month for enterprise solutions. Standalone knowledge base tools like Document360 start around $49/site/month.

Can I use knowledge base software for free?

Yes! Confluence offers a free tier for up to 10 users. Notion is free for individuals. Help Scout offers a 15-day free trial.

How do I choose the right knowledge base software?

Consider: (1) Is it for internal or external use? (2) Do you need integrated chat/support? (3) What's your budget? (4) How important is design customization? (5) Do you need AI features?

How do knowledge bases reduce support tickets?

Studies show 60-70% of support inquiries can be answered with self-service documentation. A well-organized knowledge base with good search helps customers find answers instantly, reducing ticket volume and costs.

What's the difference between internal and external knowledge bases?

Internal knowledge bases (Confluence, Notion) are for employee-only access with features like permissions and integrations. External knowledge bases (Help Scout, Zendesk Guide) are customer-facing with public search, article ratings, and support integration.
