What is Calendly?

Calendly is a scheduling automation platform that eliminates the back-and-forth of meeting scheduling. Used by over 20 million users worldwide, it allows you to share your availability and let invitees book time directly.

Whether you're a sales rep, consultant, recruiter, or educator, Calendly streamlines how you schedule meetings, demos, interviews, and consultations.

Key Features

  • Automated Scheduling: Connect calendars, set availability rules, and share booking links
  • Multiple Event Types: One-on-one, group, round-robin, and collective meetings
  • Calendar Integrations: Google Calendar, Outlook, iCloud
  • Video Conferencing: Built-in Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, GoToMeeting
  • Workflows: Automated reminders, follow-ups, and confirmations
  • Team Scheduling: Round-robin, collective, and sequential routing
  • Custom Branding: White-label booking pages
  • Routing Forms: Qualify leads before booking
