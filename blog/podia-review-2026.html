What is Podia?

Podia is a creator-focused platform that lets you sell online courses, digital downloads, and memberships all in one place. Founded in 2014, it has grown to serve thousands of creators worldwide.

What makes Podia special is its simplicity and transparency:

  • No transaction fees - Keep 100% of your revenue
  • No limits - Unlimited courses, students, and products
  • Built-in email - Native email marketing included
  • Free plan available - Start selling immediately

Podia positions itself as a "no-fee" platform - while competitors charge per-sale fees, Podia's flat pricing means you know exactly what you're paying.
