Frequently Asked Questions

Are AI presentations better than manually created ones?

AI presentations excel at speed and consistency. They can generate professional decks in minutes rather than hours. However, the best presentations still benefit from human refinement, personal touches, and strategic storytelling. AI handles the design heavy lifting so you can focus on content.

Can I export AI presentations to PowerPoint?

Most AI presentation tools offer export options including PDF, PPTX, and sometimes video. Gamma, Beautiful.ai, and Canva all support PowerPoint export. Always check export options if compatibility with traditional tools is important.

Do I need design skills to use these tools?

No - that's the point! AI presentation tools handle the design automatically. You provide content, ideas, or answers to questions, and the AI ensures everything looks professional. Even if you've never opened PowerPoint, you can create stunning presentations.

How much do AI presentation tools cost?

Prices range from free tiers (Canva, some others) to $15-20/month for professional plans. Most tools offer team/enterprise pricing with additional features like brand controls, analytics, and advanced collaboration.

Which AI presentation tool has the best templates?

Gamma offers the most modern, web-native templates. Beautiful.ai has the largest library of traditional business templates. Tome provides the most unique, interactive templates. Canva has the broadest variety across all design types.
