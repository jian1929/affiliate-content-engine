Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best landing page builder for small businesses?

Leadpages offers the best balance of features and price for small businesses. It's affordable, easy to use, and includes everything you need to start converting visitors into leads.

Do I need a landing page or a full website?

For most marketing campaigns, dedicated landing pages outperform website pages. Landing pages eliminate distractions and focus visitors on one goal, resulting in 40-60% higher conversion rates.

How many landing pages do I need?

Start with at least 3-5 landing pages targeting different campaigns or audience segments. As you scale, aim for one landing page per ad group or keyword cluster for maximum relevance and conversion rates.

Can I use WordPress for landing pages?

While WordPress can create landing pages with plugins like Elementor or Thrive Architect, dedicated landing page builders like Unbounce offer superior conversion optimization features and faster page speeds.

What's the average conversion rate for landing pages?

Average landing page conversion rates range from 3-5%. High-performing landing pages with proper optimization can achieve 10-15% or higher. Unbounce customers report an average lift of 48% after optimization.
