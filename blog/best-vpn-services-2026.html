Frequently Asked Questions

Is using a VPN legal?

VPNs are legal in most countries including the US, UK, Canada, and EU. Some countries like China, Russia, and Iran restrict or ban VPN use.

Can a VPN be traced?

Your ISP and websites can't see your activity when using a VPN, but your VPN provider theoretically could. Choose a provider with a verified no-logs policy.

Do free VPNs work?

Free VPNs often have data limits, slower speeds, and may sell your data. Premium VPNs like NordVPN and Surfshark offer excellent value with money-back guarantees.

Will a VPN slow down my internet?

All VPNs add some overhead, but premium services minimize speed loss to 10-20%. Some VPNs like ExpressVPN are actually faster than your ISP for streaming.

Can I use a VPN on multiple devices?

Most VPNs support 5-7 devices. Surfshark offers unlimited simultaneous connections. All major VPNs have apps for Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android.
