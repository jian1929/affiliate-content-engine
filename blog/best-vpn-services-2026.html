Frequently Asked Questions

Are VPNs legal?

Yes, VPNs are legal in most countries. However, using a VPN for illegal activities remains illegal. Some countries (China, Russia, UAE) restrict VPN usage.

Do VPNs slow down internet speed?

A quality VPN like NordVPN or ExpressVPN may slow speeds by 10-30% depending on server location and protocol. Some VPNs (ExpressVPN) are so fast you won't notice the difference.

Can I use a free VPN?

Free VPNs are not recommended. They often log your data, show ads, limit bandwidth, and may even sell your information. Paid VPNs offer much better security and privacy.

Which VPN is best for Netflix?

NordVPN, ExpressVPN, and CyberGhost all reliably unblock Netflix and other streaming services. ExpressVPN is our top pick for streaming due to its MediaStreamer feature.

Can I use one VPN account on multiple devices?

Yes, all VPNs allow multiple simultaneous connections. Surfshark offers unlimited devices, while others offer 5-7 connections per account.
