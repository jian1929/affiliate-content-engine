Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most accurate translation software?

DeepL consistently ranks as the most accurate machine translation service, particularly for European languages. Its neural AI produces more natural, contextually appropriate translations than competitors.

Is Google Translate good enough for business?

For basic internal communication, Google Translate can work. However, for customer-facing content, marketing materials, or legal documents, professional human translation or services like DeepL Pro are recommended.

How much does DeepL Pro cost?

DeepL Pro starts at $24/month for the Advanced plan (5 users, unlimited translation) and $74/month for Ultimate (unlimited users and features). The free version has usage limits.

Can AI translation replace human translators?

AI translation has improved dramatically but still struggles with nuance, cultural context, idioms, and highly specialized content. For professional use, post-editing by human translators remains recommended.

What is the difference between translation and localization?

Translation converts text from one language to another. Localization adapts content for a specific region, including cultural references, units, date formats, and local regulations. Localization is broader than translation.
