What is Teachable?

Teachable is one of the most popular online course platforms, powering over 100,000 courses and serving millions of students worldwide. Founded in 2013, it has become the go-to choice for beginners due to its remarkably intuitive interface.

Unlike more complex platforms, Teachable prioritizes simplicity. You can have your first course live in under an hour, without touching a single line of code.
