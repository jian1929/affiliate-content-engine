Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best POS system for a small restaurant?

Toast is our top recommendation for restaurants due to its purpose-built features including kitchen display systems, table management, and menu engineering tools designed specifically for food service operations.

How much does Square POS cost?

Square POS is free for basic use with just transaction fees (2.6% + 10 cents per card). Paid plans start at $60/month for Plus, adding advanced features like item kits, libraries, and custom amounts.

Do I need payment processing with POS?

Most POS systems require you to use their payment processing (Square, Toast, Shopify Payments), though some like Lightspeed allow third-party processors at higher rates. Bundled solutions typically offer better value.

Can I use POS offline?

Square and Shopify POS offer offline mode - you can continue processing transactions without internet and they'll sync when connectivity returns. Toast requires constant connectivity for most features.

What's the difference between retail and restaurant POS?

Restaurant POS includes table management, course sequencing, kitchen display integration, and tipping support. Retail POS focuses on inventory management, customer profiles, and barcode scanning.
