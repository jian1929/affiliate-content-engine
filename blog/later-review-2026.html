What is Later?

Later is a visual-first social media management platform founded in 2014, specializing in Instagram scheduling and visual content planning. With over 7 million users, Later has become the preferred tool for Instagram-first creators, brands, and agencies who prioritize aesthetics in their social media strategy.

What sets Later apart is its drag-and-drop visual calendar that lets you see exactly how your Instagram feed will look before publishing. This "visual planning" approach helps brands maintain cohesive, aesthetically pleasing feeds that attract and retain followers.

Does Later work with TikTok?

Yes, Later supports TikTok scheduling. You can schedule and auto-post TikTok videos directly from Later's dashboard.

Can I use Later for free?

Later doesn't offer a free plan, but provides a 14-day free trial on all paid plans so you can test the platform thoroughly.

Does Later auto-post to Instagram?

Yes, Later supports automatic posting to Instagram—no manual intervention required. You can also enable First Comment automation for hashtags.

Can I plan my entire Instagram feed with Later?

Absolutely! Later's visual calendar shows your upcoming posts in a grid view, letting you see and rearrange your entire feed before anything goes live.

Does Later have a link-in-bio feature?

Yes, Later includes a customizable link-in-bio page with multiple button links, shoppable Instagram feeds, and built-in analytics to track clicks.
