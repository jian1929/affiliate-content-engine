Key Features Deep Dive

1. Webinar Rooms

WebinarJam lets you create multiple "rooms" for different webinar series or products. Each room has its own registration page, thank you page, and replay settings. This is perfect for businesses running multiple concurrent webinar campaigns.

2. HD Broadcasting

Unlike some competitors that compress video heavily, WebinarJam delivers HD broadcasting with screen sharing, multiple camera angles, and presentation overlays. The video quality is consistently praised in user reviews.

3. Automated Webinars

The hybrid webinar feature lets you upload a pre-recorded video and broadcast it live. You can add live chat overlay, trigger polls at specific times, and even have team members join to answer questions in real-time.

4. Engagement Tools

  • Chat moderation — Filter, pin, and highlight messages
  • Private chat — 1:1 communication with attendees

  • Hand raising — Let attendees signal they have a question
  • CTA buttons — Floating call-to-action buttons with timers

5. SMS & Email Notifications

Send SMS reminders 1 hour and 15 minutes before the webinar. Combined with email sequences, this creates multiple touchpoints to maximize attendance rates.

6. Recording & Replays

All webinars are automatically recorded. You can offer replays for a limited time (e.g., 30 days) to capture registrants who couldn't attend live. Replay pages can include different CTAs than the live version.
