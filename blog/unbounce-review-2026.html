What is Unbounce?

Unbounce is a cloud-based landing page builder that has been at the forefront of conversion optimization since its founding in 2010 in Vancouver, Canada. The platform was created specifically to solve a critical problem that marketers faced: the inability to create, test, and optimize landing pages without relying on developers or IT teams. Over the past 15+ years, Unbounce has evolved from a simple drag-and-drop editor into a comprehensive conversion platform that powers millions of landing pages for businesses of all sizes.

At its core, Unbounce allows marketers to build high-converting landing pages without writing a single line of code. The platform provides access to over 200 conversion-optimized templates that have been designed based on years of data and experimentation. These templates are not just aesthetically pleasing—they are engineered to guide visitors toward specific actions, whether that's signing up for a newsletter, downloading an ebook, booking a demo, or making a purchase. Every element, from button placement to form fields to headline hierarchy, has been carefully considered to maximize conversion rates.

What sets Unbounce apart from traditional website builders like WordPress, Wix, or Squarespace is its laser focus on conversion optimization. While general website builders are designed to create complete websites, Unbounce is purpose-built for one thing: turning visitors into customers. This specialization means that every feature, every template, and every tool has been optimized with conversion in mind. The platform includes built-in A/B testing, real-time analytics, dynamic text replacement for personalized experiences, and advanced targeting options that allow you to show different variations to different audiences.

In 2026, Unbounce continues to innovate with its AI-powered Smart Builder, which can generate conversion-focused landing pages from simple text prompts in minutes rather than hours. This positions the platform as an essential tool for digital marketers, PPC advertisers, e-commerce businesses, and agencies who need to rapidly create and test landing pages at scale. Whether you're running a small campaign or managing hundreds of landing pages for multiple clients, Unbounce provides the tools and infrastructure to maximize your conversion rates and ROI.

Key Features

Unbounce offers a comprehensive suite of features designed specifically for conversion-focused marketing. Let's dive deep into the most important capabilities that make Unbounce the go-to choice for serious marketers.

Drag-and-Drop Page Builder

The heart of Unbounce is its intuitive drag-and-drop page builder that requires no coding knowledge whatsoever. The builder provides a visual canvas where you can add, move, resize, and customize elements with simple mouse interactions. Every element—images, text blocks, buttons, forms, videos, countdown timers, and more—can be dragged into position and customized to match your brand. The builder also includes alignment guides and snap-to-grid functionality to ensure your pages look professional and polished. Advanced users can access the HTML/CSS editor for custom modifications, while beginners can achieve professional results using only the visual tools.

200+ Conversion-Focused Templates

Unbounce's template library is one of its most valuable assets. With over 200 professionally designed templates, you have access to proven layouts that have been tested and optimized for conversions across various industries and use cases. Templates are categorized by goal (lead generation, webinar registration, product launch, etc.) and industry, making it easy to find a starting point that matches your specific needs. Each template is fully customizable, so you can maintain your brand identity while benefiting from conversion-optimized layouts. The templates are also mobile-responsive, ensuring your pages look great on any device.

A/B Testing and Split Testing

Conversion rate optimization is impossible without testing, and Unbounce makes A/B testing seamless. Built into every plan, the platform allows you to create multiple variations of your landing pages and test them against each other. You can test anything—headlines, images, copy, button colors, form fields, pricing displays—and let real data determine which version performs best. Unbounce provides statistical significance calculations to help you know when you have enough data to make a confident decision. This data-driven approach to optimization is what separates Unbounce from simple page builders.

Popups and Sticky Bars

Beyond full landing pages, Unbounce allows you to create high-converting popups and sticky bars that can be deployed across your entire website. These overlay elements are incredibly effective for capturing leads, promoting special offers, and increasing engagement. You can configure when and where these elements appear—whether on exit intent, after a certain time spent on page, after scrolling a certain percentage, or on specific pages. This targeting capability ensures you're showing the right message to the right visitor at the right moment in their journey.

Dynamic Text Replacement

For PPC marketers, Dynamic Text Replacement (DTR) is a game-changing feature. DTR automatically adjusts your landing page text to match the search query that brought the visitor to your page. If someone searches for "affordable CRM software" and clicks your ad, your landing page can automatically display that headline. This creates a seamless experience between the ad and the landing page, improving Quality Scores in Google Ads and reducing bounce rates. Unbounce makes DTR easy to implement with simple variable tags that you can insert into your page content.

AMP Landing Pages

In an era where mobile performance directly impacts search rankings and user experience, Unbounce offers Accelerated Mobile Pages (AMP) for lightning-fast load times on mobile devices. AMP landing pages load almost instantly, which can significantly improve your bounce rates and conversion rates on mobile traffic. This is particularly valuable for time-sensitive campaigns where every second of load time costs you conversions.

Integrations

Unbounce connects seamlessly with the marketing tools you already use. Native integrations with major CRM platforms (Salesforce, HubSpot), email marketing services (Mailchimp, Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign), advertising platforms (Google Ads, Facebook Pixel), analytics tools (Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager), and dozens of other services mean you can add Unbounce to your workflow without disrupting existing processes. For tools without native integrations, Unbounce offers webhook support and a robust API for custom connections.

Unbounce AI: Smart Builder

Unbounce's introduction of AI capabilities through its Smart Builder represents a significant leap forward in landing page creation. In 2026, the AI-powered features have matured into a powerful tool that can dramatically accelerate your page creation workflow while maintaining the conversion focus that Unbounce is known for.

The Smart Builder works by analyzing your input—describing what you want to achieve—and then generating a complete, conversion-optimized landing page in minutes. You provide information about your product or service, your target audience, and your conversion goal, and the AI generates a structured page with headlines, subheadings, body copy, call-to-action buttons, form fields, and supporting images. The generated pages are based on Unbounce's extensive data about what works in conversion optimization, incorporating proven patterns and best practices automatically.

What makes the Smart Builder particularly valuable is its ability to handle the heavy lifting of page creation while still giving you full control to customize the results. You can accept the AI-generated content as-is, modify specific sections, or regenerate individual elements until they match your vision. The AI also suggests improvements based on conversion best practices, such as recommending shorter form fields, stronger CTAs, or more compelling headlines. For marketers who need to create dozens of landing pages for different campaigns or audience segments, the Smart Builder can reduce creation time from hours to minutes.

Beyond page generation, Unbounce's AI extends to other optimization features. The platform can analyze your existing pages and provide recommendations for improvements based on visual and content analysis. As you run A/B tests, AI algorithms can help identify winning variations faster and suggest next test hypotheses. This ongoing optimization assistance, combined with the initial page generation, makes Unbounce one of the most AI-forward landing page platforms available today.
