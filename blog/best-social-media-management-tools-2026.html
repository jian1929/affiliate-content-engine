Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free social media management tool?

Buffer offers the best free plan with 3 social accounts, 10 scheduled posts per account, and basic analytics. It's perfect for individuals and small businesses just getting started with social media marketing.

Can I manage multiple accounts with one tool?

Yes, all social media management tools allow you to connect and manage multiple accounts across different platforms. The number of accounts typically depends on your pricing plan.

Do these tools support TikTok?

Yes, Buffer, Hootsuite, and Sprout Social support TikTok scheduling. Later does not currently support TikTok.

Can I schedule Instagram posts automatically?

Yes, all major tools support automatic Instagram posting. You can schedule posts, carousels, and Stories to publish automatically without manual intervention.

What about social listening?

Hootsuite and Sprout Social offer the most comprehensive social listening features. Buffer and Later do not include dedicated social listening capabilities.

Which tool is best for agencies?

Hootsuite and Sprout Social are the best choices for agencies due to their client management features, white-labeling options, and comprehensive reporting. Buffer also offers an Agency plan.
