The Bottom Line

Both platforms excel in different areas. For digital products and maximum commissions, ClickBank is the clear winner. For physical products and established brands, ShareASale offers more options.

Many successful affiliates use both platforms to diversify their income streams and access products across categories.

Our recommendation: Start with ClickBank for instant access to high commissions, then add ShareASale as you expand into physical products.
