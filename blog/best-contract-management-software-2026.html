Frequently Asked Questions

What is contract management software?

Contract management software (CLM) helps businesses create, sign, store, and manage contracts throughout their lifecycle. It automates reminders, tracks obligations, and ensures compliance with contract terms.

Do I need CLM if I already use DocuSign?

DocuSign handles e-signatures well but has basic contract storage features. If you need advanced contract organization, renewal tracking, obligation management, and analytics, dedicated CLM software adds significant value.

How much does contract software cost?

Prices range from free (basic e-signature) to $50+/user/month for enterprise CLM. ContractWorks offers simple storage starting at $499/year, while enterprise platforms like Agiloft and Ironclad require custom pricing.

Is DocuSign legally binding?

Yes, DocuSign e-signatures are legally binding in over 180 countries, including the US (ESIGN Act) and EU (eIDAS). They meet strict compliance requirements for court-admissible evidence.

What's the difference between e-signature and CLM?

E-signature focuses on getting signatures on documents. CLM (Contract Lifecycle Management) handles the entire contract process from creation to renewal, including storage, obligations, and analytics.
