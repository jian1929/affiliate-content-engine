What is Hootsuite?

Founded in 2008, Hootsuite is the world's most widely used social media management platform, serving over 200,000 organizations globally—from small businesses to Fortune 500 companies. Hootsuite provides enterprise-grade tools for scheduling, analytics, social listening, team collaboration, and security that scale with your business.

Unlike simpler tools, Hootsuite is built for teams that need to manage multiple brands, coordinate complex approval workflows, and maintain brand consistency across hundreds of social accounts. Their extensive integration ecosystem and robust API make it a cornerstone of enterprise social media operations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Hootsuite offer a free plan?

No, Hootsuite doesn't have a free plan. However, they offer a 30-day free trial on all paid plans so you can test the platform before committing.

How many social accounts can I manage?

This depends on your plan. Professional allows 10 profiles, Team allows 20, Business allows 35, and Enterprise offers unlimited profiles.

Can I use Hootsuite for Instagram?

Yes, Hootsuite fully supports Instagram including scheduling, stories, and direct publishing. You can manage multiple Instagram accounts from the same dashboard.

Is Hootsuite secure for enterprise use?

Absolutely. Hootsuite is SOC 2 Type II certified, offers single sign-on (SSO), granular permission controls, data residency options, and audit logs—all essential for enterprise security requirements.

Can agencies use Hootsuite?

Yes! The Enterprise plan includes white-label reporting, client management features, and dedicated support perfect for agencies managing multiple client accounts.
