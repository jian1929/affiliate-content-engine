Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free CRM software?

HubSpot offers the best free CRM with genuinely useful features including contact management, deal tracking, email tracking, and more. Zoho CRM and Freshsales also offer free tiers, though with more limited functionality.

How much does CRM software cost?

CRM pricing ranges from free (HubSpot, Zoho) to $165+ per user per month for enterprise solutions like Salesforce. Most CRMs charge per user monthly, with prices typically ranging from $12-50 per user for standard plans.

Can I migrate data between CRMs?

Yes, all major CRMs support data import. HubSpot and Pipedrive offer migration tools. Salesforce has comprehensive data import wizards. Most CRMs also support CSV imports for manual data transfer.

Which CRM is easiest to implement?

Pipedrive and HubSpot are the fastest to implement, often taking just a few days. Salesforce enterprise implementations can take months and often require consultants.

Do these CRMs integrate with other tools?

Yes, all major CRMs integrate with popular tools. Salesforce has the largest ecosystem with 5,000+ apps on AppExchange. HubSpot offers native integrations with 1,000+ tools. Zoho integrates seamlessly with other Zoho products.

Which CRM is best for small businesses?

HubSpot's free CRM or Zoho CRM are best for small businesses. Both offer genuinely useful free tiers and affordable paid plans that scale with your business.

What CRM features do I really need?

Essential CRM features include contact management, deal/pipeline tracking, email integration, task management, and basic reporting. As your business grows, you may need advanced automation, AI insights, and custom reporting.
