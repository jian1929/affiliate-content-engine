Introduction to Moz Pro

Moz Pro stands as one of the most established names in SEO software, founded in 2001 by Rand Fishkin and his mother Gillian. With over 15 years of accumulated domain authority data, Moz offers something unique in the crowded SEO tool market: historical perspective and one of the largest link index databases available.

In 2026, Moz continues to differentiate itself through its proprietary metrics, particularly Domain Authority (DA) and Page Authority (PA), which have become industry-standard benchmarks for evaluating website credibility and ranking potential.

Moz Keyword Explorer

Moz's Keyword Explorer distinguishes itself through its unique Keyword Difficulty metric that considers both page authority and domain authority of top-ranking competitors. This gives more accurate difficulty scores than simple backlink counts used by competitors.

The tool offers:

  • Accurate monthly search volume estimates
  • Organic CTR predictions
  • Priority score combining volume, difficulty, and CTR
  • Keyword suggestions with SERP features identification
  • Bulk analysis for up to 1,000 keywords

Link Building Tools

Moz's link research capabilities are particularly strong, offering one of the cleanest link indexes in the industry. The Spam Score metric, developed using machine learning on millions of sites, helps identify potentially dangerous link opportunities.

Link Building Features

  • Link Intersect: Find sites linking to competitors but not you
  • Spam Score: Predicts likelihood of a domain being penalized
  • Link Tracking: Monitor new and lost backlinks over time
  • Anchor Text Analysis: Understand your link profile composition
  • Link Opportunities: AI-powered recommendations for link building
