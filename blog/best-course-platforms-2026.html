How to Choose the Right Course Platform

Selecting the best course platform depends on your specific situation. Here are the key factors to consider:

1. Budget

If you're just starting and have a limited budget, Thinkific's free plan is unbeatable. You can launch your first course with zero cost. As you grow, you can upgrade to paid plans for advanced features.

2. Marketing Needs

If you want everything in one place, Kajabi is your best choice. It includes email marketing, landing pages, and automations that would cost hundreds monthly elsewhere. If you prefer to use separate tools, Teachable or Thinkific with integrations work well.

3. Audience Reach

If you want instant access to millions of students without marketing, Udemy is the way to go. However, be prepared to accept lower revenue per sale and limited pricing control.

4. Ease of Use

Teachable offers the best balance of power and simplicity. New users can launch quickly while having room to grow into advanced features.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which course platform is easiest to use?

Teachable and Thinkific are both extremely beginner-friendly. Teachable has a slight edge in its intuitive dashboard, while Thinkific offers more flexibility in its free plan.

Can I use my own domain with these platforms?

Yes, all major course platforms support custom domains. Thinkific offers this even on their free plan, while Teachable and Kajabi require paid plans for custom domain support.

How much can I earn as an affiliate for course platforms?

Most course platforms offer 30% recurring commissions for affiliates. If you refer a customer paying $99/month, you earn approximately $30/month for as long as they stay. High-performing affiliates can earn thousands monthly.

Do I need to be a tech expert to create courses?

No! All the platforms reviewed here are designed for non-technical users. You can create professional courses using video uploads, PowerPoint presentations, and quizzes without any coding knowledge.

Can I offer course bundles and upsells?

Yes, all platforms support some form of bundling. Thinkific has the most flexible bundling options, while Kajabi includes advanced upsell capabilities in its all-in-one platform.

Which platform is best for selling to businesses (B2B)?

Kajabi and Teachable have better B2B features including team management, bulk enrollments, and invoice billing. Thinkific also offers business plans with additional features.
