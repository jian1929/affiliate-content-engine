What Is ActiveCampaign?

ActiveCampaign is a customer experience automation platform (CXA) that combines email marketing, marketing automation, sales automation, and CRM tools into one powerful platform. Trusted by over 150,000 businesses worldwide, it's designed for companies that need sophisticated automation without enterprise-level complexity.

Unlike basic email tools, ActiveCampaign focuses on helping businesses automate the entire customer journey—from first contact to loyal customer. Its strength lies in its automation builder, which lets you create complex workflows that respond to customer behavior in real-time.

Why Growing Businesses Choose ActiveCampaign

ActiveCampaign strikes the perfect balance between power and usability. It's sophisticated enough for complex marketing teams but accessible enough for small businesses. The platform's automation capabilities consistently outperform competitors in benchmark studies, with users reporting 20-40% improvements in conversion rates after switching.

The Automation Experience

ActiveCampaign's automation builder is where it truly stands out. Unlike simple drip campaign tools, you can create multi-step automations that branch, loop, and respond dynamically to customer actions.

For example, you can create an automation that: welcomes new subscribers, waits 3 days, sends a product recommendation based on their signup source, tracks if they open and click, follows up with a discount if they don't purchase, and tags them as a "warm lead" when they do.
