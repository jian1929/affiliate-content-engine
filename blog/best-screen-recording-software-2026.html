Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best screen recording software for beginners?

Loom is our top recommendation for beginners due to its incredibly intuitive interface, one-click recording, and instant sharing capabilities. The free tier is generous enough for most casual use cases.

Is OBS Studio really free to use?

Yes, OBS Studio is completely free, open-source software. There's no hidden costs, watermarks, or premium versions. It's used by professional streamers and content creators worldwide.

Can I record just my screen without the camera?

Yes, all screen recording software allows you to record screen only, camera only, or both simultaneously. Most platforms let you toggle these on/off before or during recording.

What's the difference between screen recording and screencasting?

Screen recording captures your screen as a video file. Screencasting often implies real-time streaming or broadcasting, though the terms are used interchangeably for recorded content.

Do I need a microphone to record my screen?

Not necessarily, but audio narration significantly improves video quality. Most tools let you record system audio, microphone audio, or both. Some allow adding audio later in editing.
