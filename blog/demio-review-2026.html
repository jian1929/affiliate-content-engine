Key Features of Demio

1. Hybrid Webinars (Automated + Live)

Demio's hybrid approach lets you upload a pre-recorded presentation and stream it live while engaging with real-time chat and Q&A. This gives you the polish of a recording with the energy of a live event.

2. Automated Follow-Up Sequences

Unlike many competitors, Demio has built-in email automation. You can create multi-step follow-up sequences triggered by attendee actions: registered, attended, missed, or took specific actions during the webinar.

3. Registration Pages

Demio provides customizable registration pages with countdown timers, social sharing, and timezone detection. You can also embed registration forms on your own site.

4. Engagement Scoring

See exactly how engaged each attendee was with engagement scores based on chat participation, poll responses, and time spent watching. This helps prioritize follow-ups for hot leads.

5. Replay Availability

Set replay windows so registrants can watch the recording within a specific timeframe after the live event. This captures leads who couldn't attend live.
