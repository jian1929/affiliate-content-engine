What is Buffer?

Buffer is a cloud-based social media management platform founded in 2010, known for its simplicity, transparency, and commitment to helping businesses of all sizes manage their social media presence efficiently. With over 75,000 paying customers and a reputation for excellent customer support, Buffer has become one of the most trusted names in social media management.

Unlike complex enterprise solutions, Buffer focuses on making social media management accessible to everyone—from solo entrepreneurs to growing agencies. Their platform emphasizes ease of use, beautiful design, and honest business practices.

Publish & Schedule

Analytics & Reporting

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Buffer offer a free trial?

Yes! Buffer offers a 14-day free trial on all paid plans. There's also a permanent free plan with basic features.

Can I use Buffer for Instagram?

Yes, Buffer supports Instagram scheduling. Note that Instagram requires a connected Facebook page for direct publishing.

Does Buffer work for teams?

Absolutely. Buffer's Team and Agency plans include collaboration features like role-based access, approval workflows, and shared content libraries.

Can I schedule TikTok posts with Buffer?

Yes, Buffer supports TikTok scheduling. You can schedule posts and receive reminders when it's time to publish.
