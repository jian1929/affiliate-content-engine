What is ConvertKit?

ConvertKit is an email marketing platform specifically designed for creators. Unlike generic email tools, ConvertKit is built around the unique needs of bloggers, podcasters, YouTubers, course creators, and newsletter writers who want to build and monetize their audience.

Founded in 2013 by Nathan Barry, ConvertKit has grown to serve over 700,000 creators worldwide. The platform's philosophy is simple: help creators turn casual readers into loyal, paying subscribers.

Key Differentiator: ConvertKit doesn't try to be everything to everyone. Instead, it focuses 100% on creator needs — simple automation, easy opt-in forms, and powerful monetization tools.

Key Features

1. Visual Email Automations

Create complex email sequences without needing technical skills. The visual automation builder uses a flowchart-style interface where you can drag and drop triggers, actions, and conditions.

2. Smart Forms & Landing Pages

Build beautiful opt-in forms and landing pages with ConvertKit's built-in tools. Choose from templates optimized for conversions or build custom designs.

3. Creator-Focused Landing Pages

Dedicated landing pages for:

  • Lead magnets and freebies
  • Email course signups
  • Webinar registrations
  • Product launches

4. Broadcasts (One-Time Emails)

Send newsletter-style emails to your full list or segments. ConvertKit's broadcast tool is straightforward — no confusing segments or complex rules.

5. Digital Products & Courses

ConvertKit Commerce lets you sell:

  • Digital downloads (PDFs, ebooks, templates)
  • Email courses
  • Community subscriptions
  • One-time products

6. Reporting & Analytics

Track opens, clicks, subscriber growth, and revenue. The dashboard gives you a clear view of your email marketing performance.
