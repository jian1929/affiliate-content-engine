What is MailerLite?

MailerLite is an email marketing platform designed for simplicity and affordability. Founded in 2005, it has evolved into a full-featured solution that competes with much more expensive tools while maintaining an easy-to-use interface.

What makes MailerLite stand out is its focus on removing friction. The platform is straightforward — no unnecessary complexity, no overwhelming features, just solid email marketing that works.

Key Point: MailerLite's free plan lets you have up to 1,000 subscribers and send 12,000 emails per month. This is one of the most generous free tiers in the industry.
