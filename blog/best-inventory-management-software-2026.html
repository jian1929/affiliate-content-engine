Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best inventory management software for small business?

Zoho Inventory is our top recommendation for small businesses due to its generous free tier, excellent integrations, and seamless connection to the Zoho ecosystem at an accessible price.

Do I need inventory management software?

If you sell physical products and struggle to keep track of stock levels, orders, or suppliers, inventory software eliminates guesswork, prevents stockouts, and saves time on manual counting.

How much does inventory software cost?

Prices range from free (Sortly, Zoho Inventory) to $500+/month for enterprise solutions. Most small business options fall between $30-100/month with essential features included.

Can inventory software integrate with my e-commerce store?

Yes, most modern inventory software integrates with Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, eBay, and other platforms to automatically sync stock levels and orders across all channels.

What's the difference between inventory management and warehouse management?

Inventory management tracks stock levels, locations, and movements. Warehouse management adds fulfillment processes like pick, pack, ship, and advanced logistics optimization.
