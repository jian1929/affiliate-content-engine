What is Shopify?

Shopify is the world's leading e-commerce platform, powering over 4.5 million active stores and processing over $540 billion in total merchant volume since its founding in 2006. From solo entrepreneurs to Fortune 500 companies, Shopify provides everything you need to sell online.

Unlike traditional website builders, Shopify is purpose-built for commerce. Every feature—from product listings to checkout—is designed to convert visitors into customers. The platform handles hosting, security, and payment processing so you can focus on your business.

Getting Started with Shopify

Starting your Shopify store takes less than 30 minutes:

  1. Sign up: Enter your email, password, and store name
  2. Add products: Upload images, set prices, manage inventory
  3. Choose a theme: Free and paid themes available
  4. Customize: Add your branding, colors, and content
  5. Connect a domain: Buy through Shopify or connect existing
  6. Set up payments: Enable Shopify Payments or choose gateway
  7. Launch: Remove password protection and start selling
