What is ClickUp?

ClickUp is a cloud-based project management and productivity platform that aims to replace all your other tools. Launched in 2017, it has rapidly grown to become one of the most popular project management solutions, serving millions of teams worldwide.

What sets ClickUp apart is its philosophy: "One app to replace them all." Rather than juggling multiple tools for tasks, docs, goals, and communication, ClickUp consolidates everything into a single, highly customizable workspace.

Key Point: ClickUp's free plan is incredibly generous — unlimited tasks, unlimited members, and all core features included. This makes it accessible for teams of any size.

Key Features

1. Customizable Tasks

Tasks in ClickUp are extremely flexible. Add custom fields, statuses, priorities, assignees, due dates, and more. Create custom task types for different work items.

2. Hierarchical Structure

Organize work in a hierarchy that makes sense:

  • Workspace → Top level, your organization
  • Spaces → Major categories or teams
  • Folders → Group related projects
  • Lists → Individual projects
  • Tasks → Individual work items
  • Subtasks → Break down tasks further

3. Docs & Wikis

Create rich-text documents, wikis, and knowledge bases directly in ClickUp. Embed tasks, link to other docs, and create a central knowledge repository.

4. Goals & OKRs

Set company, team, and personal goals. Track progress with measurable targets and key results. Link tasks to goals for automatic progress tracking.

5. Time Tracking

Built-in time tracking with:

  • One-click timers
  • Timesheets
  • Estimates vs actuals
  • Integrations with Harvest, Toggl

6. Integrations & Automations

Connect ClickUp with 1,000+ apps. Build custom automations to eliminate repetitive work. Native integrations with GitHub, Slack, Google Drive, and more.

Multiple View Types

ClickUp's killer feature is its multiple view types. See the same data in different ways:

List View

Classic task list with grouping, filtering, and sorting. Great for sprint planning and backlog management.

Board (Kanban) View

Drag-and-drop columns for workflow stages. Perfect for visual workflow management.

Box (Calendar) View

Calendar-based view showing tasks by due date. Essential for scheduling and deadline management.

Mind Map View

Visual brainstorming with connected nodes. Great for project planning and ideation.

Gantt View

Timeline-based project planning with dependencies. Perfect for project managers.

Workload View

See team capacity and distribution. Prevent burnout and balance workloads.

Activity View

Social feed of team activity. Stay informed without meetings.
