What is SpyFu?

SpyFu is a competitive keyword research tool that lets you spy on your competitors' most profitable keywords, ad campaigns, and SEO strategies. Unlike traditional keyword tools that start with your own data, SpyFu flips the script — it starts with your competitors and works backward.

Who Should Use SpyFu?

  • SEO Professionals looking to reverse-engineer competitor rankings
  • PPC Advertisers wanting to analyze competitor ad spend
  • Affiliate Marketers seeking high-converting keywords
  • Small Business Owners competing against established players

Key Features That Make SpyFu Powerful

Spy on Competitor Keywords

Enter any domain and instantly see every keyword they've bought on Google Ads (both organic and paid). You'll see their estimated ad spend, average CPC, and how long they've been targeting each keyword.

Ad History Checker

See every version of ads your competitors have run going back to 2006. Watch their ad copy evolve over time and identify patterns that work.

Keyword Research Tools

Get keyword suggestions, search volume, cost-per-click estimates, and competition level. SpyFu goes deeper — it shows you which keywords competitors rank for organically, giving you a roadmap of proven opportunities.

SERP Analysis

See who's ranking for any keyword, their estimated traffic, and how many backlinks they have. Perfect for finding gaps in your own strategy.
