Key Features Deep Dive

1. The Scheduling Engine

EverWebinar's scheduling engine is its crown jewel. Instead of showing all available times, it uses behavior-based logic to show the "next best" webinar time based on:

  • Visitor's local timezone
  • Time of day they arrive
  • Conversion history for different time slots
  • Availability and urgency signals

This means each visitor sees a personalized webinar schedule that maximizes the likelihood of registration.

🎯 The JustCall Integration (Game-Changer)

EverWebinar integrates directly with JustCall to let visitors book phone calls during the webinar. This creates a high-value conversion path for prospects who want to talk before buying. The call is automatically scheduled, and CRM fields are pre-populated — no manual data entry required.

2. Chat Simulation System

One of the biggest challenges with automated webinars is creating engagement. EverWebinar solves this with a sophisticated chat simulation system:

  • Pre-scheduled messages — Messages appear at specific timestamps during the webinar
  • Live Q&A — Real questions get routed to your team for response
  • Social proof notifications — "157 people are viewing" creates urgency
  • Comment highlighting — Pin important messages for all to see

3. Conversion-Optimized Replay Pages

Some registrants will miss the live session. EverWebinar creates separate, conversion-optimized replay pages with:

  • Different CTAs than the live version
  • Progress bar showing "You missed it live"
  • Time-limited availability
  • Exclusive bonuses for watching the replay

4. Multi-Webinar Support

Run multiple automated webinar campaigns simultaneously, each with:

  • Unique registration pages
  • Separate email sequences
  • Different products or offers
  • Individual analytics

5. Built-in Email Sequences

EverWebinar includes email automation for:

  • Registration confirmation
  • 24-hour reminder
  • 1-hour reminder
  • 15-minute reminder
  • Post-webinar follow-up (attended)
  • Post-webinar follow-up (missed)
