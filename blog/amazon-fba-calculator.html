How the Calculator Works

Amazon FBA fees can be complex. Here's the breakdown of every fee considered in this calculator:

1. Referral Fees

Amazon's commission on each sale, typically 6-15% depending on category.

Referral Fee = Selling Price × Category Rate (usually 15%)

2. Fulfillment Fees

Amazon charges for picking, packing, and shipping. Rates based on weight:

4. Additional Costs to Consider

  • Prep fees — $0.30-0.60 per unit for labeling

  • Unplanned services — $0.20-2.50 if Amazon has to prep

Profit Calculation Formula

Revenue = Selling Price × Units Sold Total Costs = Product Cost + Shipping + Referral Fee + Fulfillment Fee + Storage + Other Net Profit = Revenue - Total Costs

How to Improve Your Margins

1. Negotiate Product Costs

Even a 10% reduction in product cost can significantly boost margins. Build relationships with suppliers and order larger quantities.

2. Optimize Shipping

Compare LTL vs. parcel shipping. For heavier products, LTL can be significantly cheaper.

3. Reduce Weight

Lighter products have lower fulfillment fees. Consider packaging redesign to reduce weight and size.

4. Adjust Pricing

Small price increases have outsized impact on profit. A 5% price increase on 25% margins = 20% profit increase.

5. Focus on High-Margin Products

Use tools like Helium 10 or Jungle Scout to find products with inherently higher margins.
