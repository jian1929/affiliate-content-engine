What is Stripe?

Stripe is the leading online payment processing platform trusted by millions of businesses worldwide. Founded in 2010 by Irish brothers Patrick and John Collison, Stripe has revolutionized how businesses accept and manage payments online.

Key Fact: Stripe processes hundreds of billions of dollars annually for companies ranging from solo entrepreneurs to Fortune 500 enterprises. It's the payment processor of choice for tech-forward businesses.

Unlike traditional payment processors with complex setups and hidden fees, Stripe offers a developer-friendly API, transparent pricing, and a suite of tools that make accepting payments seamless. Whether you're running a SaaS subscription service, an e-commerce store, or a marketplace, Stripe provides the infrastructure to handle it all.

Key Features of Stripe

1. Stripe Payments

The core product: accept payments anywhere on the web. Features include:

  • Stripe Elements: Pre-built, customizable UI components for card capture
  • Payment Methods: Cards, wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), bank transfers, and more
  • Instant Payouts: Get paid within minutes to your debit card
  • Multi-currency: Accept and settle in 135+ currencies
  • Smart Retry Logic: Automatically retry failed payments with optimal timing

2. Stripe Billing (Subscriptions)

Built for SaaS businesses and any company with recurring revenue:

  • Flexible billing models (usage-based, tiered, per-seat)
  • Trial management and dunning (automated collections)
  • Proration and plan changes mid-cycle
  • Invoice generation and management
  • Tax compliance with Stripe Tax

3. Stripe Connect (Marketplaces)

Powerful platform infrastructure for marketplaces and platforms:

  • Connect sellers to your platform in minutes
  • Automatic splits between platform and sellers
  • Compliance handled by Stripe
  • Payout to sellers on your timeline

4. Stripe Radar (Fraud Prevention)

Machine learning-powered fraud protection:

  • Blocks fraudulent transactions before they happen
  • Customizable rules for your risk tolerance
  • $0 cost for fraud (Stripe covers fraudulent chargebacks)

5. Stripe Checkout

One-click, conversion-optimized checkout pages:

  • Works across devices with mobile-first design
  • Supports 20+ languages
  • Reduces cart abandonment significantly
  • Automatic address and card validation

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Stripe safe and secure?

Yes. Stripe is PCI DSS Level 1 compliant (the highest level). They handle all security certification, tokenization, and encryption. Your customers' payment data never touches your servers.

How long does it take to get started?

You can start accepting payments within minutes. Basic integration can take an hour; full customization with Stripe Elements may take a few days.

Does Stripe hold funds?

Stripe may place reserves or holds on accounts deemed higher risk, similar to all payment processors. These are typically temporary for new accounts or can occur after unusual activity.

What happens if a customer disputes a charge?

You'll receive a chargeback notification. Stripe provides tools to dispute with evidence, and with Radar's fraud protection, fraudulent chargebacks cost you nothing.

Can I use Stripe for subscription billing?

Absolutely. Stripe Billing is one of the most powerful subscription management tools available, supporting trials, multiple billing intervals, proration, and automatic retries.

Does Stripe work with WordPress/Shopify/etc.?

Yes. Stripe has plugins for all major platforms including WooCommerce, Shopify (native), Easy Digital Downloads, MemberPress, and more.
