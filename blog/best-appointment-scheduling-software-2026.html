Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free appointment scheduling tool?

Yes! Calendly offers a free plan with 1 event type and unlimited bookings. Square Appointments is free if you use Square payments. YouCanBook.me and Setmore also offer free tiers.

What is the best scheduling software for small business?

For most small businesses, Calendly or Acuity Scheduling are the best choices. Calendly for professionals, Acuity for businesses needing payments and packages.

How do I choose appointment scheduling software?

Consider: (1) Do you need payments? (2) What calendar do you use? (3) Team size? (4) Integration needs? (5) Budget? (6) Industry-specific features?

What scheduling software has the best affiliate program?

Calendly offers 25% recurring commission for the first year. Setmore offers 20% recurring. These are competitive rates in the scheduling space.

Can I use scheduling software with Zoom?

Yes! Most scheduling tools integrate with Zoom. Calendly offers native Zoom integration on all plans, automatically creating meeting links when bookings are made.

Do I need SMS reminders?

SMS reminders significantly reduce no-shows. Acuity and YouCanBook.me offer SMS reminders on their paid plans. Consider this if no-shows are an issue.
