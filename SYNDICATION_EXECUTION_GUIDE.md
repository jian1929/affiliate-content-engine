# The Complete Amazon PPC Guide for 2026: Everything You Need to Know

Amazon PPC (Pay-Per-Click) advertising is one of the most powerful tools available to sellers looking to boost their product visibility and drive sales. With over 3 billion monthly visitors to Amazon, advertising on the platform gives your products exposure to a massive, purchase-ready audience.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll cover everything you need to know about Amazon PPC in 2026, from basic concepts to advanced optimization strategies.

## What is Amazon PPC?

Amazon PPC is an advertising model where sellers bid on keywords to have their products displayed prominently in search results and product pages. You only pay when a shopper clicks on your ad, making it a performance-based marketing channel with measurable ROI.

### Types of Amazon Ads

#### 1. Sponsored Products
The most common ad type, appearing in search results and on product pages. These ads target specific products and keywords.

**Best for:** Individual product promotion, keyword targeting

#### 2. Sponsored Brands
Feature your brand logo, headline, and up to 3 products. Appear at the top of search results.

**Best for:** Brand awareness, showcasing product lines

#### 3. Sponsored Display
Reach shoppers on and off Amazon based on interests and shopping behavior.

**Best for:** Retargeting, reaching new audiences

## Setting Up Your First Campaign

### Step 1: Choose Campaign Type
Start with Sponsored Products for most products. They're easier to manage and typically have better ROAS.

### Step 2: Select Targeting
- **Keyword Targeting:** Target shoppers searching for specific terms
- **Product Targeting:** Target shoppers viewing specific products or categories

### Step 3: Set Your Budget
Start with a daily budget of $10-20 per campaign. You can adjust based on performance.

### Step 4: Set Your Bids
Start with Amazon's recommended bid. Monitor and adjust based on performance.

## Keyword Match Types

Understanding match types is crucial for bid efficiency:

| Match Type | Example | When to Use |
|------------|---------|-------------|
| Broad | women's running shoes | Maximum reach, requires negative keywords |
| Phrase | "women's running shoes" | Balance of reach and relevance |
| Exact | [women's running shoes] | Maximum control, highest relevance |

## Negative Keywords

Negative keywords prevent your ads from showing for irrelevant searches. Add negatives regularly to improve spend efficiency.

**Common Negative Keywords for PPC:**
- "used"
- "refurbished"
- "bulk"
- "wholesale"
- "for parts"

## Optimizing Your Campaigns

### 1. Review Search Terms Weekly
Check which keywords are driving sales and which are wasting budget.

### 2. Adjust Bids Based on Performance
- Increase bids for keywords with ACoS below target
- Decrease bids for underperforming keywords
- Pause keywords with no sales after 2 weeks

### 3. Use Automatic Targeting
Let Amazon find converting keywords, then add manually to your campaigns.

### 4. Test Different Ad Creative
Try different product images and titles to see what resonates.

## Understanding ACoS

ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales) is your key metric:

```
ACoS = (Ad Spend / Ad Revenue) × 100
```

**Example:**
- Ad Spend: $50
- Ad Revenue: $200
- ACoS = 25%

### Target ACoS by Category
- High-margin products: 15-25% ACoS
- Competitive categories: 25-35% ACoS
- New product launches: 30-50% ACoS (acceptable)

## Advanced Strategies

### 1. Campaign Structure
Organize campaigns for better control:

```
├── Brand Campaign
│   └── Exact match brand keywords
├── Competitor Campaign
│   └── Product targeting competitors
├── Category Campaign
│   └── Category + feature keywords
└── Long-tail Campaign
    └── Specific, low-competition keywords
```

### 2. Bid Adjustments by Placement
Increase bids for top-of-search placement (typically 50-100% increase).

### 3. Use Portfolios
Group campaigns for easier management and budget allocation.

### 4. Implement Dayparting
Adjust bids based on when your products convert best.

## Common PPC Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not using negative keywords** - Wastes budget on irrelevant searches
2. **Setting bids too low** - Miss out on valuable impressions
3. **Ignoring search terms** - Missing optimization opportunities
4. **Too many campaigns** - Makes management difficult
5. **Not reviewing regularly** - Performance changes over time

## Tools for Amazon PPC

Consider using these tools to streamline your campaigns:

- **Helium 10:** Keyword research, PPC automation
- **Sellics:** Campaign management, analytics
- **Perpetua:** AI-powered campaign optimization
- **DataHawk:** Analytics and optimization

## Conclusion

Amazon PPC is essential for any seller looking to scale their business. Start with Sponsored Products, focus on keyword optimization, and always monitor your ACoS. With the right strategy, Amazon PPC can be a profitable channel that drives sustainable growth.

# Amazon Repricing Strategies That Actually Win the Buy Box in 2026

The Buy Box is the holy grail of Amazon selling. Studies show that over 89% of Amazon sales go through the Buy Box, making it essential for any serious seller to understand and implement effective repricing strategies.

In this guide, we'll explore the most effective Amazon repricing strategies that actually work in 2026.

## What is the Buy Box?

The Buy Box appears on the right side of a product detail page and allows customers to add items to their cart with a single click. Only one seller can win the Buy Box at a time (though multiple sellers can be featured).

### Buy Box Eligibility Requirements

To even be eligible for the Buy Box, you must:
- Have a Professional selling plan
- Maintain acceptable seller performance metrics
- Have items in stock
- Have a competitive price
- Ship items yourself (FBM) or use FBA

## The 6 Core Buy Box Factors

Amazon evaluates multiple factors when determining Buy Box winner:

| Factor | Weight | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Price | 25% | Your price relative to competitors |
| Availability | 20% | Stock levels and fulfillment speed |
| Fulfillment | 20% | FBA vs FBM performance |
| Rating | 15% | Seller rating and feedback |
| History | 10% | Account tenure and performance |
| Inventory | 10% | Consistent stock availability |

## Repricing Strategy Types

### 1. Competitive Match

The simplest strategy: match the lowest competitor price.

**Pros:**
- Easy to implement
- Always competitive
- Low maintenance

**Cons:**
- Can lead to race to bottom
- May not cover all costs

### 2. Beat-by-Percentage

Automatically undercut competitors by a set percentage (e.g., $0.01 or 1%).

**Pros:**
- More controlled than competitive match
- Still wins Buy Box
- Prevents price wars

**Cons:**
- May not be aggressive enough
- Still subject to competition

### 3. Floor-Protected Repricing

Only reprice within your minimum and maximum price boundaries.

```
Minimum Price = Your Cost + Target Margin
Maximum Price = Competitor Average × 1.2
```

**Pros:**
- Protects margins
- Prevents underpricing
- Sustainable long-term

**Cons:**
- May lose Buy Box if floor is too high
- Requires accurate cost data

### 4. Time-Based Repricing

Adjust prices based on time of day, day of week, or season.

**Best times to reprice:**
- Prime Day
- Black Friday/Cyber Monday
- Seasonal peaks

### 5. Stock-Based Repricing

Adjust prices based on your inventory levels.

**Strategy:**
- High stock → Competitive pricing
- Low stock → Higher pricing
- Last few units → Premium pricing

### 6. AI-Powered Repricing

Use machine learning to optimize prices in real-time.

**Factors considered:**
- Competitor prices
- Your inventory
- Sales velocity
- Time of day
- Seasonal trends
- Historical data

## Setting Up Your Repricer

### Step 1: Define Your Floor

Calculate your minimum acceptable price:

```
Floor = (Product Cost + Fulfillment Cost + Referral Fee + Target Margin)
```

### Step 2: Define Your Ceiling

Set a maximum price to prevent overpricing:

```
Ceiling = Buy Box Price × 1.15
```

### Step 3: Choose Repricing Rules

Configure your repricing behavior:

- Price adjustment percentage
- Reprice frequency
- Competitor filters
- Time-based rules

### Step 4: Monitor and Adjust

Review performance weekly and adjust rules as needed.

## Best Practices for Repricing

1. **Never reprice below your cost**
   - Use floor protection always

2. **Filter out bad competitors**
   - Exclude sellers with poor ratings
   - Exclude sellers with slow shipping

3. **Consider FBA vs FBM**
   - FBA sellers have advantage
   - Factor this into your strategy

4. **Don't reprice too frequently**
   - 15-30 minute intervals recommended
   - Avoid price oscillation

5. **Use multiple repricing strategies**
   - Different products may need different approaches

## Common Repricing Mistakes

1. **No floor protection** → Selling at a loss
2. **Repricing too frequently** → Price oscillation
3. **Not filtering competitors** → Competing with bad sellers
4. **Ignoring stock levels** → Running out during sales
5. **Not monitoring results** → Missing optimization opportunities

## Tools for Amazon Repricing

Popular repricing tools include:

- **Ecommerce Ops Suite:** Automated repricing with floor protection
- **Helium 10:** Repricer integrated with full toolkit
- **Sellics:** Advanced repricing features
- **RepricerApp:** Dedicated repricing solution

## Conclusion

Winning the Buy Box requires a strategic approach to repricing. Start with floor-protected competitive repricing, monitor your results, and gradually implement more sophisticated strategies as you learn what works for your products.

The key is finding the balance between winning the Buy Box and maintaining healthy margins. Automated repricing tools can help you achieve this balance while saving countless hours of manual price monitoring.

# The Ultimate Guide to Amazon Competitor Intelligence in 2026

In the competitive world of Amazon selling, knowledge is power. Understanding your competitors—their pricing strategies, product positioning, and inventory levels—can mean the difference between thriving and barely surviving.

This comprehensive guide covers everything you need to know about Amazon competitor intelligence.

## What is Competitor Intelligence?

Competitor intelligence is the systematic process of gathering, analyzing, and acting on information about your competitors and market conditions. For Amazon sellers, this means tracking:

- Competitor pricing
- Product listings
- Inventory levels
- Review accumulation
- Keyword rankings
- Advertising strategies

## Why is Competitor Intelligence Important?

### 1. Price Optimization
Stay competitive without starting price wars. Know when to adjust and when to hold.

### 2. Product Development
Identify gaps in the market and opportunities for new products.

### 3. Strategic Planning
Make informed decisions based on data, not guesswork.

### 4. Risk Mitigation
Spot threats before they become problems.

## Key Metrics to Track

### Pricing Metrics

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|--------|-------------------|
| Buy Box Price | Current market price |
| Lowest FBA Price | Aggressive competitor pricing |
| Lowest FBM Price | Cost-focused sellers |
| 30-Day Price Trend | Seasonal patterns |

### Inventory Metrics

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|--------|-------------------|
| Stock Level | If competitor is running low |
| Stockout Frequency | Vulnerable periods |
| Restock Timing | Predictable availability |

### Listing Metrics

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|--------|-------------------|
| Review Count | Market share estimation |
| Star Rating | Quality perception |
| Image Quality | Professional standards |
| Description Length | Content depth |

### 1. Manual Research

- Search for your keywords

## Competitive Response Playbook

### Scenario: Competitor drops price 10%+

**Your Options:**

1. **Match the price** (if profitable)
2. **Hold price** (if margin is thin)
3. **Improve other factors** (reviews, listing quality)
4. **Target different keywords**

### Scenario: Competitor runs out of stock

**Your Options:**

1. **Temporarily raise price** (capture premium)
2. **Increase ad spend** (capture traffic)
3. **Improve ranking** (long-term advantage)

### Scenario: New competitor enters market

**Your Options:**

1. **Evaluate their strategy** (low-price leader?)
2. **Differentiate** (quality, bundle, reviews)
3. **Monitor closely** (track their moves)

## Advanced Intelligence Techniques

### 1. Reverse Engineer Listings

Study top performers:
- Title structure

- Backend keywords
- Image approach

### 2. Track Keyword Rankings

Monitor where competitors rank for target keywords using tools like Helium 10 or Ecommerce Ops Suite.

### 3. Analyze Review Sentiment

Read competitor reviews:
- What do customers praise?
- What do they complain about?
- What improvements do they suggest?

### 4. Monitor Advertising

Use tools to see competitor ad presence:
- Which keywords are they bidding on?
