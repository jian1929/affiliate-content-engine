Frequently Asked Questions

What is the easiest e-commerce platform for beginners?

Shopify is the easiest platform for beginners. It handles hosting, security, and provides everything you need to start selling quickly. The interface is intuitive, and you can launch a store in under an hour.

Which e-commerce platform is cheapest?

WooCommerce has the lowest starting cost (free) since you only pay for hosting and domain. However, factor in the technical time required. Among all-in-one platforms, Squarespace and Wix offer competitive pricing at $27/month.

Can I switch e-commerce platforms later?

Yes, but it's not always easy. Most platforms offer migration tools or services. BigCommerce and Shopify make it relatively straightforward. WooCommerce migrations are common but require careful planning.

Which platform is best for large catalogs?

BigCommerce and Shopify Plus handle large catalogs (100,000+ products) well. WooCommerce can handle large catalogs but requires robust hosting. Magento is the enterprise choice for millions of SKUs.

Do I need to pay transaction fees?

It depends on the platform and payment processor. Shopify charges 0.5-2% transaction fees unless you use Shopify Payments. BigCommerce charges no transaction fees. WooCommerce and others don't charge platform fees.
