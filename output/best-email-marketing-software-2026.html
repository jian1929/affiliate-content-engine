Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best email marketing software for beginners?

Mailchimp's free plan is excellent for beginners due to its intuitive interface, generous free tier, and extensive documentation. However, if you're a content creator, ConvertKit's creator-focused design may be better suited to your needs.

Which email marketing platform has the best deliverability?

Deliverability varies based on list quality and sender reputation. However, Sendinblue and AWeber consistently receive high marks for inbox placement rates. All platforms will perform well if you follow email marketing best practices.

Can I switch email marketing platforms easily?

Yes, most platforms offer migration tools or services to import your contacts and email templates. ConvertKit and Mailchimp are particularly known for smooth migration processes. Just ensure your subscriber data is properly formatted before import.

Do all email marketing platforms offer automation?

Most modern platforms include automation, but the complexity varies. Basic automations like welcome emails are available on free/low-tier plans, while advanced workflows like customer journey mapping typically require higher-tier plans.

How much does email marketing software cost?

Costs range from free (Mailchimp, Sendinblue) to $100+ per month for enterprise plans. The key cost factor is usually the number of contacts, not emails sent. Consider your growth trajectory when choosing a platform.
