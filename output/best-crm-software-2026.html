Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free CRM for small business?

HubSpot CRM and Zoho CRM both offer excellent free tiers. HubSpot's free plan includes contact management, deal tracking, and email templates. Zoho's free plan supports 3 users and includes workflow automation. Choose based on your team size and growth plans.

How much does a CRM system cost?

CRM costs range from free to $300+ per user per month for enterprise solutions. Most CRMs charge per user, with prices typically ranging from $12-$50/user/month for standard features. Consider the total cost including implementation and training.

Can I migrate data from my current CRM?

Yes, most CRM platforms offer migration tools or services. Salesforce and HubSpot have dedicated migration tools, while others offer CSV import capabilities. Professional migration services are often available for complex data sets.

Do I need CRM if I have a small team?

Absolutely! CRMs help small teams stay organized, track customer interactions, and scale efficiently. Starting with a CRM early builds good habits and makes growth smoother. HubSpot and Zoho offer free tiers perfect for small teams.

What's the difference between CRM and sales force automation?

CRM is broader, encompassing all customer relationships across marketing, sales, and service. Sales force automation (SFA) is a subset focused specifically on automating sales tasks like lead management, quoting, and order processing.
