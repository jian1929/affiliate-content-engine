## Email Templates

### Template 1: General Affiliate Application
```
Subject: Affiliate Partnership Application

Hi Affiliate Team,

I'm reaching out to apply for your affiliate program. I run a content
website focused on [YOUR NICHE] that receives [TRAFFIC] monthly visitors.
I've created comprehensive reviews and comparison articles for tools in
this space.

My website: [YOUR SITE URL]
Traffic source: Organic search (Google)
Primary audience: [TARGET AUDIENCE]

I primarily promote through:
- In-content affiliate links
- Comparison articles
- Product reviews

Would love to partner with [BRAND] and help drive quality referrals.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR EMAIL]
```

### Template 2: High-Traffic Website
```
Subject: Affiliate Partnership - High-Traffic [NICHE] Website

Hello,

I'm the publisher of [SITE NAME], a [NICHE] resource site with 10,000+
monthly visitors from organic search. Our content focuses on helping
professionals choose the right software tools.

I'd like to apply for your affiliate program. We already have articles
covering [RELATED TOOLS] and would love to add [BRAND] to our
recommendations.

Website: [YOUR SITE URL]
Monthly traffic: [X] unique visitors
Traffic sources: 85% organic Google, 15% social

Our audience is [DESCRIPTION] and we're seeing strong interest in
[SUBJECT AREA].

Looking forward to partnering!

Best,
[YOUR NAME]
```
