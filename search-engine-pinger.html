📚 How It Works

What is sitemap ping?

When you publish new content, search engines don't know about it immediately. By pinging their sitemap submission services, you tell them "Hey, my sitemap has been updated!"

What happens next?

After pinging, search engines will:

1. Fetch your sitemap within hours (vs. weeks normally)
2. Crawl your new pages
3. Index your content in search results

Manual submission (for faster results)

For the fastest indexing, also submit manually at:
Google Search Console → URL Inspection → Request Indexing
Bing Webmaster → Submit Sitemap
